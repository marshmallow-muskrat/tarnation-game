# Tarnation audio provenance

Every WAV in this directory is an original short-form audio asset authored for Tarnation and
generated deterministically by [`scripts/generate-audio.mjs`](../../scripts/generate-audio.mjs).
No third-party recording, sample pack, performer, trademark, or external license is used.

The generator is retained as the source of truth so the shipped files can be regenerated and audited.
The assets are intentionally compact, stylised, and loop-safe; they are not a placeholder claim for
unlicensed music or field recordings.
